About DRKVSRIT

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Today

🏛️ Campus View

Dr. K.V. Subba Reddy Institute of Technology is a premier engineering institution committed to providing quality education and fostering innovation. Established with a vision to create competent engineers and technologists, our institution has consistently maintained high academic standards.

The institute is approved by AICTE, New Delhi and affiliated to Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University, Ananthapuramu. With state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and industry collaborations, we prepare our students for successful careers in engineering and technology.

Our commitment to excellence is reflected in our accreditations from NAAC and NBA, making us one of the preferred choices for engineering education in the region.
